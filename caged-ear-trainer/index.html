Main instrument

How it fits together. A scale gives the notes of a key. Stacking every other note of the scale gives its chords. Each chord can be played in five places on the neck, the CAGED shapes, and every shape contains small three-note triads. Click any note on a neck or a piano to hear it: the same pitch lights up on the other instrument.
